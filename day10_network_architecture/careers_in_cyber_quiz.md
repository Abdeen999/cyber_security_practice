# TryHackMe: Careers in Cyber

## Room Summary
Completed the "Careers in Cyber" room on TryHackMe. This room explores various job roles in cybersecurity, their responsibilities, and what kind of personality fits best in each role.

## Quiz Result
After taking the interactive quiz, my suggested cybersecurity role is:
**Incident Responder**

### Why Incident Responder?
*   I enjoy reacting to problems quickly and stopping them from spreading.
*   I think about what an attacker might do next.
*   I like ensuring the team has everything they need to succeed.

## Key Responsibilities of an Incident Responder
*   Create and maintain a detailed incident response plan.
*   Respond to potential incidents and protect the organization's reputation, finance, and operations.
*   Write reports after incidents and prepare for future attacks by learning from what happened.
